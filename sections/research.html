Research questions

  1. 01

    Which combinations of team composition, task type, and AI collaborator roles yield the best outcomes?

  2. 02

    Which AI collaborator roles improve inclusion (participation equality and perceived inclusion) without harming outcome quality?

  3. 03

    How does transparency about AI identity and uncertainty affect trust calibration and downstream team outcomes?

Benchmark tasks

Our project analyses how team composition, task type, AI collaborator roles, and AI transparency shape collective intelligence processes and outcomes through the following two benchmark tasks.

Problem-solving

Teams embrace a divergent-to-convergent approach by first generating a wide range of ideas, then clustering and synthesizing them into the best final solution.

Decision-making

Teams deliberate and debate a complex, value-laden question and produce a final decision with justification, e.g., in the format of an argumentative essay.
