Prolific

About

Prolific is a leading online research platform that provides advanced participant recruitment infrastructure and study design capabilities for large-scale online experiments. Within the academic community, Prolific is distinguished by its expansive global participant pool, which enables researchers to achieve highly diverse and representative sampling across various cultural backgrounds, demographics, and professional contexts.

The platform’s robust technical infrastructure allows for the reliable scheduling of complex study designs and provides sophisticated filtering mechanisms to reach specific demographic targets. By prioritising data quality, verified participant engagement, and ethical compensation, Prolific has established itself as a foundational platform for researchers conducting scalable, robust, and inclusive behavioural and social science studies.
