About

NetMind.AI is a UK-based AI technology company providing an integrated platform for building and deploying AI-powered applications. Its infrastructure combines scalable access to distributed GPU computing with flexible model APIs supporting large language models, multimodal AI, and agentic systems, enabling efficient development, fine-tuning, and deployment of intelligent solutions.

Designed as an “AI platform for builders and businesses”, NetMind.AI supports end-to-end AI workflows: from training and inference to system integration; while also offering tools and services for developing multi-agent and context-aware AI applications. Through its expertise in scalable AI infrastructure and agent-based systems, the company supports both academic research and real-world deployment of advanced AI technologies.
